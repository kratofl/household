# Code of Conduct

Household aims to be a friendly open-source project for people building and running software at home.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially with new contributors.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Be clear when discussing tradeoffs, risks, and limitations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after a maintainer asks you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or restrict participation when needed to keep the project healthy. If you see behavior that violates this code, contact a maintainer privately with relevant links or context.
